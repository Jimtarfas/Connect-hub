Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our company, products, and services.

About the Company

Who is ConnectHub Electronics Limited?

ConnectHub Electronics Limited is an Irish-registered private company limited by shares, registered under the Companies Act 2014 (CRO No. 814488). We specialise in supplying certified refurbished smartphones across Ireland and the European Union.

Where is your company based?

Our registered office is at The Black Church, St. Mary's Place, Dublin 7, D07 P4AX, Ireland. All operations and customer service are managed from Ireland.

Are you a legitimate business?

Yes - ConnectHub Electronics Limited is fully registered and compliant. Our company details can be verified on the Irish Companies Registration Office (CRO) public register at www.cro.ie using company number 814488.

Products & Quality

What does "refurbished" mean?

A refurbished device has been previously owned, returned to a certified facility, professionally inspected, repaired if needed, cleaned, and tested to ensure full functionality. Each device passes our 72-point quality inspection before sale.

What grading system do you use?

We grade devices into four categories: Grade A+ (Like New - virtually no signs of use), Grade A (Excellent - minor signs of use), Grade B+ (Very Good - light wear), and Grade B (Good - visible wear but fully functional).

Are accessories included?

All devices come with a charging cable and quick-start guide. Original boxes and headphones are typically not included unless specifically stated.

What is the battery health?

All refurbished iPhones and Samsung devices have a guaranteed minimum battery health of 85%. Batteries falling below this threshold are replaced before sale.

Orders & Payment

How can I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), as well as PayPal and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through encrypted payment gateways.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. We use industry-leading payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) and never store full payment details on our servers. Our website uses 256-bit SSL encryption.

Do prices include VAT?

Yes - all listed prices include VAT at the applicable rate. VAT invoices are provided automatically with every order.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to all 27 EU member states, plus Norway, Switzerland, and the UK. See our Shipping Information page for full details.

How long does delivery take?

Within Ireland: 1-3 working days. Within the EU: 3-7 working days depending on destination. All orders placed before 14:00 IST are typically dispatched the same day.

Returns & Warranty

What is your return policy?

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal under EU law - no questions asked. Defective items can be returned at any time within the 12-month warranty period. See our Returns Policy for details.

How long is the warranty?

Every device comes with a 12-month commercial warranty against manufacturing defects, in addition to your statutory consumer rights. See our Warranty Policy.
