Behind the Scenes

Our Refurbishment Process

Every device passes through our certified Dublin facility where trained technicians follow a strict 72-point inspection and restoration process.

01

Diagnostic Testing

Each device is connected to our proprietary diagnostic systems for a full hardware audit - covering CPU, GPU, sensors, motherboard, battery cycle count, and all connectivity modules.

  • Functional testing across 72 checkpoints
  • Battery health verification (minimum 85%)
  • IMEI & activation lock check
02

Component Repair & Replacement

Faulty parts (screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras, speakers) are replaced with high-grade certified components by our trained technicians in static-controlled workstations.

  • OEM-grade or premium aftermarket parts only
  • Anti-static workstation environment
  • Calibration of replaced screens & cameras
03

Deep Clean, Sanitise & Data Wipe

Devices are professionally cleaned, polished, and sanitised. We perform a full factory data wipe to ISO 27040 standards, ensuring no previous data remains on the device.

  • Ultrasonic cleaning of internal components
  • UV-C sanitation of external surfaces
  • Certified data destruction (ISO 27040)
04

Quality Certification & Packaging

A second technician performs the final 72-point check. Devices that pass are graded (A+, A, B+, B), packaged in 100% recyclable protective materials, and shipped to your door with full tracking.

  • Independent second-tech verification
  • Condition graded & documented
  • Eco-friendly packaging with cable & SIM tool
